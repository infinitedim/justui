New Ink and Paper landing and 404. The landing shows every part on a runner in your preset, cuts part 07 into the files it writes, and compares the two presets side by side. A new components page at `/docs/components` lists all 33 parts, each linking to its docs with a copyable `justui add` command; the 404 finds the parts closest to the path you asked for. The theme toggle turns the page like paper, the components catalog and the Theme Studio are removed (catalog URLs redirect to the components page and the part docs, Studio URLs to the landing), and docs pages gain search.
